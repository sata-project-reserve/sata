# SATA Transparency Audit Buyer Packet

Status: buyer-invoice-request-packet

This packet helps a legitimate buyer request a chairman-reviewed invoice for the SATA Transparency Audit. It does not approve work, request payment, provide a payment address, move assets, or create a delivery commitment.

## Offer

- Service: SATA Transparency Audit
- Price: $249
- Turnaround target: 48 hours after approved payment or escrow terms
- Deliverable: authority, supply, LP ownership or lock evidence, reserve-claim, disclosure, and risk-language review with concise report and machine-readable JSON

## Best Fit

- Crypto teams with public authority, liquidity, reserve, ownership, or disclosure claims
- Projects that want a factual public-evidence review without promotion or market claims
- Operators who can provide token, website, public profile, and claims to review
